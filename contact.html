Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register for an FDP program?

You can register by filling out the contact form on this page with "FDP Registration" as your subject, or by emailing us directly at aadc@adamasuniversity.ac.in. Registration details for each specific program are also provided on the Programs page.

Are certificates provided after program completion?

Yes! All participants who successfully complete an AADC program receive a jointly issued certificate from AADC, Adamas University, and the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) — a recognised mark of academic distinction.

Can faculty from external universities participate?

Absolutely. AADC programs are open to faculty and staff from all universities and higher educational institutions across India. We encourage participation from diverse institutional backgrounds.

How do I receive updates about upcoming programs?

You can follow us on social media or send us your email via the contact form to be added to our mailing list. We announce all upcoming FDPs, workshops, and events through email and social channels.

How long does it take to get a response after contacting AADC?

Our team typically responds within 24 working hours. For urgent matters, we recommend calling the main office directly during working hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
