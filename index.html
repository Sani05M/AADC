About AADC

The Academic and Administrative Development Centre (AADC) is a collaborative initiative between Adamas University and the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). Dedicated to reforming the academic fabric of higher education, we focus on empowering educators, academic leaders, and administrative officers with modern methodologies, technological integrations, and cognitive skills.

Aligned with the goals of NEP 2020, AADC translates research and academic innovations into practical pedagogy, building a bridge between traditional wisdom and global modern educational standards.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To emerge as a premier national hub for the professional grooming of higher education faculty and administrative leaders.
To foster an environment of continuous learning, modern research methodology, and academic rigor in Indian institutions.
To enable classrooms to transition smoothly into student-centric, digital-native knowledge environments.

Our Mission

Conducting cutting-edge Faculty Development Programs (FDP) focusing on outcomes-based education.
Structuring administrative development programs (SDPs) to optimize university management and leadership styles.
Promoting multi-disciplinary learning, Indian culture and sciences (IKS), and research ethics guidelines.
