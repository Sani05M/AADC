About Adamas University

Adamas University, established in 2014, is a premier institution located in Kolkata, West Bengal. The university has rapidly emerged as a center of academic and research excellence, offering undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across multiple disciplines.

The institution is dedicated to nurturing innovation, fostering interdisciplinary research, and building future-ready professionals capable of contributing to societal development and global progress.

The Centre

Academic and Administrative Development Centre (AADC)

The Academic and Administrative Development Centre (AADC) at Adamas University, in collaboration with the Association of Indian Universities (AIU), is dedicated to empowering educators, researchers, academic leaders, and administrative professionals through continuous learning, innovation, and professional development initiatives.

AADC serves as a transformative platform for enhancing teaching excellence, research capabilities, leadership development, and academic effectiveness. The centre actively promotes modern pedagogical practices, interdisciplinary learning, technology integration, and academic innovation aligned with the vision of NEP 2020 and global educational standards.

Key Functions of AADC

👨‍🏫

Professional Development Programs

Offering workshops, FDPs, seminars, and training sessions focused on modern teaching methodologies, curriculum enhancement, and assessment practices.

🔍

Research Support

Providing academic guidance, research resources, publication support, funding awareness, and interdisciplinary research opportunities.

👔

Leadership & Management Training

Developing leadership, administrative, governance, and institutional management skills among faculty and academic professionals.

💡

Innovation in Teaching

Encouraging blended learning, digital education, online teaching strategies, and technology-integrated pedagogical practices.

🤝

Mentorship & Peer Support

Creating collaborative academic environments through mentorship programs, peer learning, and faculty engagement initiatives.

⭐

Academic Excellence & Lifelong Learning

Promoting continuous improvement, academic quality, skill enhancement, and lifelong professional development among educators.
