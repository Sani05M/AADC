Our Academic Programs

FDP Program

Faculty Development Program on Literature, Language and Media

An intensive program exploring literature studies, media culture, and digital humanities. Designed to enhance academic methodologies surrounding language preservation, archives, and modern scholarly storytelling.

5 Days Duration
Aug 12 - 16, 2026
View Full Program Read Report

Program Overview & Objectives

1. Introduction to Digital Humanities

The program introduces faculty to the intersections of computing and humanities disciplines, focusing on digital tools for analyzing language, archiving cultural literature, and studying media trends.

"Fostering interdisciplinary research methodologies is essential for the future of language and media studies."

2. Language, Literature & Cultural Archives

Participants explore strategies for preserving indigenous languages and translating cultural narratives into digital archives. Sessions include scholarly communication techniques and pedagogical approaches for modern classrooms.

3. Academic Methodologies

  • Advanced media studies framework analysis.
  • Qualitative and quantitative narrative assessment.
  • Integrating NEP 2020 guidelines into curriculum design.

FDP Highlight

Interdisciplinary hands-on sessions bridging modern media with classical literature.
